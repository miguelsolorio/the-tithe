The Tithe: audio sets

Five synthesized directions. The game mixes them by depth; tags show where each one is used.

70%

1. Liturgy Upper floors

Organ drones, monk chant, tolling bells. Sacred turned demonic.

2. Undertow Basement and cistern

Submerged drones, drips, whale-like groans. Cold and pressurized.

3. Viscera Flesh caves

A heartbeat you live inside. Breathing walls, wet bass pulse.

4. Dead channel Bulb flicker only

Warped music box, tape hiss, radio static. Lo-fi dread.

5. Dread strings Encounter layer

Dissonant string clusters, endless riser, braams. Cinematic tension.
